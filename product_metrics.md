Product Metrics

Conversion Metrics

Order Creation Rate

Percentage of users who create an order after adding products to the cart.

Payment Success Rate

Percentage of orders successfully paid.


Paid Orders / Created Orders × 100%

Order Completion Rate

Percentage of orders successfully delivered.


Delivered Orders / Created Orders × 100%

⸻

Operational Metrics

Average Order Processing Time

Average time between payment confirmation and shipment.

Average Delivery Time

Average time between shipment and delivery.

Cancellation Rate

Percentage of orders cancelled before delivery.


Cancelled Orders / Total Orders × 100%

⸻

Quality Metrics

Return Rate

Percentage of delivered orders returned by customers.


Returned Orders / Delivered Orders × 100%

Failed Order Rate

Percentage of orders that could not be completed due to payment, inventory, or logistics issues.


Failed Orders / Total Orders × 100%

⸻

Business Goals

* Increase Order Completion Rate
* Reduce Cancellation Rate
* Reduce Processing Time
* Improve Payment Success Rate
* Minimize Failed Orders
